Look at the top-level hypotheses in the issue tree (the branches directly under
the problem statement).

A hypothesis may be phrased either as a question ("Did conversion fall after the
price change?") or as a testable statement ("The same people arrive and fewer
convert"). Grammar is not the test. The test is whether it OPENS an
investigation or CLOSES one.

PASS if every hypothesis is something the data could confirm or kill, and none
of them picks the answer in advance.
FAIL if any hypothesis states a conclusion ("The February pricing change caused
the drop"), recommends an action ("We should roll back pricing"), or assigns
blame to a team as the finding rather than as something to test.
