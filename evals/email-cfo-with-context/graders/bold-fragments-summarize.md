how the email was built (sequencing, emotional lever, notes to the author).

Consider ONLY the email: everything from the subject line down to the sign-off.
Stop there. Any bolded labels in the commentary after the email - "Approach:",
"Emotional lever:", "Why it's built this way" - are NOT part of the email and
must be ignored completely.

Within that email, read only the bolded fragments, in order.

PASS if those fragments alone convey the recommendation, the main reasons, and
the ask.
FAIL if the bolded text inside the email is keywords or scattered phrases that
don't hold together as an argument, or if nothing in the email is bolded.
